DiskGOup is free, open source and MIT licensed. It stays that way — there is
no paid tier, no telemetry, no advertising, and no feature held back for
subscribers.

If it saved you time, cleaned up a disk, or gave you a scriptable tool you
could actually use on a server, you can support its development:

[![GitHub Sponsors](https://img.shields.io/github/sponsors/elshadaghazade?style=flat-square&label=Sponsor)](https://github.com/sponsors/elshadaghazade)

**[Sponsor the project on GitHub →](https://github.com/sponsors/elshadaghazade)**

## What the money pays for

The project has no company behind it. Sponsorship pays for the things that
are not free for an open-source project:

- **The time** — the releases, the platform-specific fixes and the bug
  reports that nobody is paying anyone to look at.
- **The infrastructure** — the CI minutes that build a macOS, a Linux and a
  Windows binary for every release.
- **The reach** — the money that is left over goes into writing the
  documentation and getting the project in front of the people who need it.

## Other ways to help that cost nothing

- **Report a bug.** A report that includes the command and its output is
  usually fixed in a day, and it is worth more than a donation.
- **Answer a question** in the [discussions](https://github.com/elshadaghazade/diskgoup/discussions)
  or on [goup.vc](https://goup.vc/).
- **Improve the documentation.** If something here was unclear, it probably
  is unclear for everyone else too.
- **Send a pull request.** The rules are in
  [Contributing](/development/contributing/).

## Where the project lives

- The community is at [goup.vc](https://goup.vc/).
- The source, the issues and the releases are on
